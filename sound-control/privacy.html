Sound Control privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026

In short: Sound Control does not collect, store, send or share any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access.

This policy explains what the Sound Control app for Android (package com.sagelab.sound.control), published by Sage Lab, does with information on your device. It takes priority over the general Sage Lab privacy policy where they differ.

What the app uses on your phone

  • Sound settings. The app reads and changes your phone's volume levels (media, call, ringtone, notifications and alarm) and applies a loudness effect when you turn boost on. This happens only on your device.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional). If Do Not Disturb is on and you change the ringer, Android asks you to grant this access. The app uses it only to change the ringer and volume levels you ask it to.
  • Notifications (optional). While boost is on, the app shows a notification with a Turn off button. It contains no personal information.
  • Your choices. The app saves your theme choice and whether it has already asked for notification permission. This stays on your device and may be included in your Android backup, which Google handles under its own privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • It does not request internet access, so it cannot send anything off your device.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, microphone, camera, files or other apps' data.
  • It does not use advertising IDs, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Feedback emails

If you choose to send feedback from the app, your email app opens with our address, your phone model and Android version filled in. You can edit or delete anything before sending. We use emails only to reply to you and to fix problems, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Children

Sound Control is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
